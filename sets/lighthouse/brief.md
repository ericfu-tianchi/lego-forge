# Brief

**Request (user's words):** A lighthouse on a small rocky island, LEGO style.

**Subject (one sentence):** A classic striped round lighthouse on a small rock island in the sea, with a glass lantern room, a gallery railing, a keeper's storehouse and two minifigures.

**Scale:** minifig — a minifig is 2×1 studs and ~13 plates. The tower is compressed to ~ 4× a minifig's height (real ~ 12–15×) so it reads as the landmark; the storehouse is one storey, the lantern is the hero detail.

**Signature features (3–5):**
1. Round tower with alternating red and white bands (white/red 4×4 round bricks).
2. Glass lantern room (trans-yellow lit glass) under a dark red dome and a cone finial.
3. Gallery plate with a railing of spindled fences and round corner posts.
4. Rock island: stepped grey rock tiers in the sea, flat top for the tower.
5. Keeper's storehouse with a pitched roof at the foot of the tower.

**Size:** footprint 24 × 20 studs (sea base) · island 18 × 14 studs at water level · tower 4 × 4 studs · height ~ 48 plates to the finial · target 300–800 parts

**Palette (8):** main White + Red (tower bands) · rock Dark Bluish Gray / Light Bluish Gray · sea Medium Azure · accents Trans Yellow (lantern), Dark Red (dome, roof), Black (railing posts? — see build)

**Out on purpose:** no door in the tower (a round wall has no real door opening at this scale), no lantern light beam, no wave detail, no cliff-side stairs. The island is a single flat-topped rock.

**From the picture (if any):** none. Proportions taken from a classic Portland / Hook Head–type tower (height ~ 3–4 × base width), compressed as above.
