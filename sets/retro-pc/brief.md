# Brief

**Request (user's words):** Build me a LEGO model of a retro 1980s desktop computer.

**Subject (one sentence):** A beige 1980s desktop PC: system unit with two floppy drives, a CRT monitor on top showing a green "C>" prompt, a keyboard and a floppy disk on a desk.

**Scale:** display — an object as a model, ~1:2.4 horizontally (case 26 studs wide), every detail built.

**Signature features:**
1. Beige case with two floppy drives (slot between bezels) and a red power switch
2. Deep CRT monitor on top: recessed black screen, green pixel text, knobs, tapering rear with vents
3. Separate stepped keyboard with spacebar, cable to the case

**Size:** footprint 34 × 36 studs (desk) · height ~43 plates · target 300–600 parts

**Palette:** main Tan · secondary Light Bluish Gray, Dark Bluish Gray, Black · accents Green (screen), Red (power) · desk Dark Tan · White (disk label)

**Out on purpose:** mouse (not 80s), printer, cabling behind, minifigs.
